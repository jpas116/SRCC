# What SRCC needs to provide

Everything the new site needs from SRCC to get from mockup to launch. Grouped by how much they unblock.

## 1. Access (nothing can launch without these)

| Item | Why | Who likely has it |
|---|---|---|
| **Domain registrar login** for srccpaart.org | Controls where the website address points. The single most important item. | Whoever registered the domain. Possibly the Wix account. |
| **Hosting login** for the current site | To take it down cleanly at launch and recover anything we missed. | The original builder. |
| **Code repository access** (probably GitHub) | Same. Ask the original builder to transfer or share it. | The original builder. |
| **Google Calendar** stays public, and someone keeps it updated | The new site reads events from it automatically. If the calendar goes stale, the site goes stale. | Whoever manages the calendar now. |
| **Zeffy admin access** (or an API key generated by an admin) | To pull events and sell tickets on the site. | SRCC's Zeffy account owner. |
| **Instagram business or creator account** login, or willingness to connect it to a feed widget | So new posts appear on the site automatically. | Whoever runs @srccpaart. |

## 2. Decisions

- **Ticketing:** SRCC has sold tickets on both Zeffy and VenuePilot. Pick one. Zeffy is free and already their donation platform; the site is designed around it.
- **Donations:** PayPal and Zeffy both exist today. Recommend Zeffy only, since it charges no fees.
- **Membership signup:** currently goes through Linktree. Should become a Zeffy form so recurring billing lives in one place.
- **Who edits the site** after launch, so the admin page is built for them.

## 3. Content with blanks in the mockup

| Page | Needed |
|---|---|
| Membership | Tier names, monthly prices, what each tier includes. FAQ answers: do I need to be a pro, how to book studio time, cancellation, how art sales work. |
| About | Founders' story in their own words (two or three paragraphs). One quote from a founder or member. Team names, roles and portraits. Sponsor and partner logos, if any. |
| Book the Space | Capacity (standing / seated), stage size, PA and backline list, load-in details, rental rates if they publish them. |
| Programs | Confirm Boundaries Lounge Jams is second Sundays and Yoga Flow is third Sundays. Price for Lounge Jams. Details on the recording, podcasting and photography setups. |
| Single event | Band links (Bandcamp, Spotify, Instagram) for upcoming shows, so "Listen" works. |

## 4. Photos (biggest visual upgrade)

The current site has flyers but almost no photographs. Ten to fifteen good ones would transform the site:

- The building from Lancaster Ave
- The stage, with and without a band
- A crowd at a Saturday show
- The Members Lounge
- Open Art Studio in progress, people painting
- The recording and podcast setups
- The founders and crew together

Phone photos are fine. Landscape orientation, well lit, no heavy filters.

## 5. Nice to have

- Band or artist names and dates for any upcoming shows not yet on the Google Calendar
- A short list of "programs we'd like to run" for the Pitch a Program section
- A newsletter tool preference (Mailchimp, Substack, or just a mailbox), if they want more than email signups
